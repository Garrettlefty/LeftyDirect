What happens after you click "Buy"

LeftyDirect doesn't hold inventory or ship anything. It's a storefront that points you to the retailer that actually has the club.

01 / Find

You find a club here

Every listing is pulled from a retailer's live feed and checked for an explicit left-hand designation before it's shown.

02 / Redirect

You check out with the retailer

Clicking "View at [retailer]" sends you to their site with your cart, payment, and shipping handled entirely by them.

03 / Commission

LeftyDirect earns a referral fee

The retailer pays LeftyDirect a small commission for the referral. Your price doesn't change either way.
